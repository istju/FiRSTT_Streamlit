# FiRSTT Project

**FiRSTT** — *Fundamental Inter-relational Reality Spacetime Theory*

## Research aim

The research aims at a spacetime model in which **time is primary**: not merely a measured coordinate, but a source-like structure from which space may emerge as a secondary, relational property.

This site’s mathematical work restores a component-form, quaternion-related reading of Maxwell’s equations — distinct from the later Heaviside–Gibbs vector reduction — and keeps three layers strictly separate:

1. **SOURCE** — original component notation  
2. **RECONSTRUCTED / UNIFIED** — clarified mathematical form  
3. **FiRSTT INTERPRETATION** — hypothesis-level reading  

## Core orientation (hypothesis level)

- Time is treated as primary; space is investigated as emergent.  
- Quaternion algebra is used as an **algebraic representation** and computational tool, not automatically as a physical four-dimensional coordinate space.  
- The FiRSTT-specific `torsion_product` is a mathematical construction; its physical interpretation (e.g. spacetime torsion) remains open.  
- Open-system thinking is preferred over idealised closed systems when discussing dynamical stability.

## What this page is — and is not

| This page **is** | This page **is not** |
|------------------|----------------------|
| A presentation of the research direction | Experimental proof of FiRSTT claims |
| A link between the Maxwell reconstruction and the broader FiRSTT questions | A claim that simulations validate the physical hypotheses |
| A reminder of the four mathematical layers (standard algebra → FiRSTT construction → numerical demo → physical hypothesis) | An endorsement of any particular experimental protocol |

Everything under the FiRSTT label beyond the explicit mathematical constructions is treated as **hypothesis**. Numerical demonstrations and internal research notes do not constitute experimental validation.

## Relation to the Maxwell reconstruction

The layered Maxwell material on this site (Symbol Registry, Equation Registry, historical background) is kept visible and source-preserving so that:

- the historical component equations remain readable,  
- notational collisions are resolved without silent rewriting,  
- and any FiRSTT-level reading stays clearly marked as interpretation, not as a mathematical consequence of the sources alone.
